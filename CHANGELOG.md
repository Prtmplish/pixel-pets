# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Pixel-art pet scene in the Explorer sidebar, drawn entirely with `fillRect` on a
  scaled-up low-resolution canvas — no image assets.
- Four pets: cat, dog, fox, ghost.
- Three scenes: autumn forest, starlit night with fireflies, snowfall.
- Click the scene to throw a ball; the pet chases it down. Click the pet for hearts.
- Idle behaviour: wanders, sits, then sleeps until you interact.
- View title bar: throw ball, choose pet, next scene, rock paper scissors.

## 0.1.0

- Rock paper scissors as a floating quick pick (`Ctrl+Alt+R`), with a persistent
  win/loss/draw record and streak tracking in the status bar.
